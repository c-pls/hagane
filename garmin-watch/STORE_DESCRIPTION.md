# Connect IQ Store listing — Hagane

## Short summary (one line)
Strength training on your wrist: follow your routines set by set, log real reps and weight, rest timers, saved to Garmin Connect.

## Full description

Hagane turns your Forerunner 255 into a gym logbook. Plan routines in the Hagane Android app, then train with just your watch — no phone needed in the gym.

FEATURES
• Routines on your wrist — exercises, sets, reps and weight sync from the Hagane phone app and work offline.
• Big, clear set screen — weight and reps in large numbers, plus live heart rate and set timer.
• Log what you really lifted — UP/DOWN adjusts reps by ±1, "Edit Weight" changes the load. Changed values are highlighted.
• Smart rest timer — a custom rest time per exercise, wrist buzzes at 10 s and the last 3 s, overtime counter, ±15 s adjustment on the fly.
• Supersets — go straight from A to B, rest after the pair.
• Busy machine? Hold UP to switch exercise, finish it, and Hagane brings you back to where you were.
• Undo or skip a set from the pause menu.
• Heart-rate recovery — see how far your heart rate dropped from the set's peak while you rest.
• Resume after exit — if the app closes mid-workout, pick up where you stopped.
• Saved to Garmin Connect — heart rate, calories and a lap for every set with exercise, reps and weight.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Create routines in the Hagane Android app.
2. Open Hagane on your watch — it syncs via Garmin Connect Mobile.
3. Choose a routine, press START, and lift.

NOTES
• Requires the Hagane Android app for creating and syncing routines.
• Garmin Connect Mobile must be installed and paired on your phone for syncing.
• Workout history is stored in Garmin Connect only — the phone app does not keep sessions.
• Exercise names are shown in English/ASCII text.

Compatible with: Forerunner 255, Forerunner 255 Music.
